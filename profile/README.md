# EditBio

**Brazilian biotechnology transforming biology into therapeutic assets.**

EditBio is a Brazilian biotechnology company that transforms genomic knowledge into solutions for human health. Through proprietary platforms, we identify and validate novel therapeutic targets and develop assets with potential to enable the next generation of advanced therapies.

## From genome to innovation

Our approach combines:

- **Functional genomics**
- **CRISPR screening**
- **Cell engineering**
- **Multi-omics analysis**
- **Functional validation**
- **Translational biology**

Integrating these capabilities allows us to connect biological discovery, experimental validation, and therapeutic asset development.

## Expertise

### Genetic engineering and functional genomics

We work from individual and multiplex target engineering to large-scale screens to validate biological mechanisms and accelerate the development of biotechnological assets.

### Bioinformatics and AI

We integrate proprietary and public functional data, bioinformatics, and artificial intelligence to identify and prioritize targets with translational potential.

### 3D cellular models

We use human- and patient-derived organoids, including genetically engineered models, to validate targets and hypotheses in biologically relevant systems.

### Biotechnology platforms

We integrate the technologies required to move from discovery to asset, connecting target identification, validation, and development.

## R&D

Our R&D program focuses on **tumor-associated macrophages**, a cell population relevant to immune suppression, tumor progression, and treatment resistance in solid tumors.

With the **Gene2Edit** platform, we seek to transform these targets into immunotherapeutic assets with potential for licensing, co-development, and strategic partnerships.

## Gene2Edit platform

The Gene2Edit pipeline integrates functional genomics, target prioritization, experimental validation, and preclinical development.

```text
LARGE-SCALE FUNCTIONAL GENOMICS
              ↓
       TARGET PRIORITIZATION
              ↓
   3D CELLULAR MODEL VALIDATION
              ↓
    SYSTEMIC TESTING & EFFICACY
              ↓
  INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY PROTECTION
              ↓
        VALIDATED ASSET
```

### 01 — Large-scale functional genomics

Large-scale functional approaches map genes, pathways, and mechanisms relevant to initiating programs with greater biological clarity.

### 02 — Target prioritization

Functional data, public evidence, and proprietary models are integrated through algorithms to identify targets with greater therapeutic potential.

### 03 — Validation

Advanced cellular models, including tumor-derived organoids, allow us to evaluate genetic alterations, cellular responses, and functional evidence before progressing to more systemic testing.

### 04 — Efficacy

Systemic testing evaluates whether effects observed in experimental models persist under more complex conditions, including animal models, supporting the assessment of efficacy, initial safety, and biological robustness.

### 05 — Protection

Evidence generated throughout the pipeline supports the protection and technological differentiation strategy for assets, methods, and results.

### 06 — Validated asset

The objective is to consolidate assets with functional, translational, and strategic evidence to guide their development, licensing, partnership, or program expansion.
